# Design System content must be original

Every Published Design System must contain only original direction or material the publisher has the right to use. AI may assist production, but human review must verify originality, rights, and accessibility before publication.

A Design System may reference broad art movements. It must not claim to replicate a living designer, company, or recognizable product. Rights review is recorded in the Design System Evaluation evidence.

The repository MIT License covers every published Design System. An installed Design Contract remains bare design direction and does not repeat license, attribution, provenance, or evaluation prose.
